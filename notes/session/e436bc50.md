### 🔧 Path Forward

**Current demos that work:**

- Replace GLFW window creation with VK_KHR_display
- Use GBM for buffer management
- Direct virtio-gpu scanout instead of windowing

The viewer is fully deployed and would work on a system with a display server, but needs refactoring for headless virtio-gpu rendering.
